CORE PRODUCT IDEA
-----------------
The original core idea is a highly precise customized handpan metronome:
- exact on-beat timing
- musical feel / musical max
- attractive animation
- easy UI
- no lag/drift at high speed
- manually editable metronome text
- complete musical note/rhythm sequence entry
- numbers, solfege, or pitch names
- rests and durations
- fractional beat positions
- loop, metronome, speed ladder
- practice and assessment

The app is not only a 9-note player. Training may eventually include playing
locations/techniques such as body hits, around-Ding hits (for example D1),
Slap and other techniques. Do not assume note number alone identifies technique.

TEXT NOTATION
-------------
Current PatternTextCodec syntax:
    NOTE@POSITION:DURATION#TECHNIQUE^HAND~VELOCITY!

Examples:
    1@0:0.5
    Bb3@0.5:1.5#GHOST_NOTE^L~0.7!
    DING@3:1
    REST@4:2

Profile-aware parsing exists. Ambiguous D is rejected where appropriate;
scientific D4 is valid; DING means central Ding.
